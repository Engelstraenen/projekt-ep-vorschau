# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and ES modules. Three.js 0.186.1, GSAP, ScrollTrigger, and Lenis are vendored under `assets/vendor/`. No build step. The site is the existing Projekt EX preview (GitHub Pages and Apache). Inferred from the repository. No interview tool was available in this session, so every fact below is taken from the repo or from the 6 Nov 2026 brief and is marked where it is not already on the page.

## Users

People evaluating Projekt EX look at fictional demo sites. On ORBIT they scroll a single preview route. The page itself says it is a demo, that ORBIT is not a real studio, and that the form stores and sends nothing.

## Product Purpose

`referenzen/orbit/` is a publishable preview of a scrollytelling studio site. Success is a desktop and mobile page a reviewer can scroll from hero to contact, with the approved ORBIT name, headline, and device, and with motion that follows the website portion of the supplied reel.

## Positioning

ORBIT is a labeled fiction inside the Projekt EX preview. It is not a client project and not the Buttermax site from the reel.

## Operating Context

The route lives with the other `referenzen/` demos. Legal pages for this demo are `referenzen/orbit/impressum.html` and `referenzen/orbit/datenschutz.html`. The form never leaves the browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Own German copy already on the page stays: headline „Ideen, die hängen bleiben.“, „Digital mit Charakter.“, navigation Studio / Arbeiten / Kontakt, „Projekt starten“, „Was soll bewegt werden?“, and the fictional works Funke, Nordlicht, and Salon.
- Feldgerät, Bake, and Spule are studio models in the same demo, not additional clients. Inferred as the way to show more than three objects without inventing customers.
- No Buttermax name, copy, or assets. No text baked into 3D screenshots.
- Do not merge or deploy until a later explicit approval. Inferred from the task, not from the page.
- No new domain, account, service, or purchase.

## Brand Commitments

ORBIT. Heavy Schibsted Grotesk, already self-hosted on the route. Approved first frame: yellow field, black wordmark and navigation pills, the line „Ideen, die hängen bleiben.“, the note „Digital mit Charakter.“, and a large black device with chrome lip and orange controls. The 6 Nov 2026 brief binds the scroll order to the reel’s website portion: pinned hero, organic hard wipe into a short glossy dark scene, sticky studio type with 3D fragments in front of and behind the type, a deep gadget grid that becomes a horizontal strip, then a dark contact with large type, a foreground gesture, a yellow splash, and the strip leaving upward.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved frame and reel notes supplied with the task. The reel file is reference material, not a page asset.
- Existing route copy, fonts, vendor libraries, and the demo disclosure.

## Product Principles

- The demo must say it is a demo.
- Motion follows the reel’s website scroll; the brand on that motion is ORBIT.
- Nothing on the page pretends a form was delivered or a client is real.
- Headlines stay sharp HTML.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing page already supports keyboard access, a skip link, reduced motion, and visible focus. Those stay. Inferred requirement from the current route plus the brief: reduced motion is a static stack without pinning and without WebGL.
