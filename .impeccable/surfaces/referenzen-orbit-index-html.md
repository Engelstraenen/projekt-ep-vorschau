Audience: people judging the Projekt EX preview. Job: scroll the fictional ORBIT studio from the yellow hero to the contact form. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one continuous scroll through five stations, the way the reel’s monitor moves, and it refuses a deck of still full-screen cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat signal yellow and near-black fields. Heavy Schibsted Grotesk. Black pills. A separate ink blob. Hard-surface devices with chrome lips and orange controls, lit by a studio environment. The dark station is glossy metal and speaker cones, not a black fade.

STORY: The visitor meets the line „Ideen, die hängen bleiben.“, passes through metal, reads the studio sentence while fragments cross it, watches the gadget collection tighten into a strip, and reaches „Was soll bewegt werden?“ with a foreground gesture. The primary action is Projekt starten.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow edge to edge. ORBIT pill, Studio, Arbeiten, Kontakt, and Projekt starten along the top. The headline sits left, large and sharp. The approved device is large, center-right, clearly dimensional, with a soft shadow. The ink blob sits behind it and moves at its own rate. „Digital mit Charakter.“ sits low. A down cue sits at the lower left.

FORM: Brief-pinned reel sequence, not a rolled concept. Seed key: brief-pinned. Signature interaction: scroll scrubs a pinned hero orbit, then an organic mask opens onto the chrome station, then fragments drift in front of and behind sticky type, then the grid collapses to a strip.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
